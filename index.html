About iBackHome

A lot of us are building back home from a flat in London, a night shift in Houston, a desk in Dubai. The photos arrive on WhatsApp, the money leaves by transfer, and the rest is hope.

iBackHome is a simple idea. A vetted engineer visits your site, takes photos in our app that cannot be faked, and you approve each stage before the next payment moves. Your builder gets paid for finished work. You see the truth.

We are not builders, and we never hold your money. Our pilot starts in the South-East, where we can know the sites, the prices and the people properly. We will expand across Nigeria soon.

The problem

What goes wrong when you cannot be there

Millions of Nigerians abroad send money home to build. Too many hear the same story: the photos look fine, the money is gone, and the walls are not where they were promised.

sound familiar?

Photos you cannot trust

Old, borrowed or staged pictures that say nothing about what is on site today.

Money ahead of progress

Requests for the next payment before the last stage is finished, or even started.

Costs that keep rising

Prices and quotes that climb with nobody to check them against the market.

No one of your own on site

Relying on someone with their own life, and no way to hold anyone to account.

How it will work

Proof first. Payment second.

This is the service we are researching. Your answers tell us if it solves the right problem, and what it should cost.

Agree the plan

You, your contractor and our team agree a bill of quantities, split into stages, before any money moves.

An engineer visits

A vetted engineer visits your site and captures tamper-proof photos with the in-app camera, location and time.

You see the proof

You review real evidence from your phone, wherever you are, and approve the stage.

Then the money moves

Funds are released to the contractor and suppliers through a licensed escrow partner, only after verification and your approval.

We are not a construction companyWe do not build. We make sure what is paid for is what is built.
