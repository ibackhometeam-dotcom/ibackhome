The problem

Building a home you cannot visit is built on trust alone.

Millions of Nigerians abroad send money home to build. Too many hear the same story: the photos look fine, the money is gone, and the walls are not where they were promised.

Photos you cannot trust

Old, borrowed or staged pictures that say nothing about what is really on site today.

Money ahead of progress

Requests for the next payment before the last stage is finished, or even started.

Costs that keep rising

Material prices and quotes that climb with nobody to check them against the market.

No one of your own on site

Relying on a relative or a friend who has their own life, and no way to hold anyone to account.

How it will work

Proof first. Payment second.

This is the service we are researching. Your answers tell us if it solves the right problem, and what it should cost.

Agree the plan

You, your contractor and our team agree a bill of quantities, split into milestones, before any money moves.

A verified supervisor

A vetted supervisor visits the site and captures tamper-proof photos with the in-app camera, location and time.

You see the proof

You review real evidence from your phone, wherever you are, and approve the milestone.

Then the money moves

Funds are released to the contractor and suppliers through a licensed escrow partner, only after verification and your approval.

We are not a construction companyWe do not build. We make sure what is paid for is what is built.
We never hold your moneyFunds sit with a licensed escrow partner, not with us.
Evidence cannot be fakedPhotos are taken in-app only, signed by the device and checked on our server.
Our vision

A home in Enugu should be as certain as a home anywhere.

We want every Nigerian abroad to build with the confidence of standing on the site themselves. Honest builders get paid fast for real work. Clients see the truth. Trust becomes something you can check, not something you hope for.
